# Code of Conduct

AIVV's community aims to be welcoming and respectful. Participants should communicate constructively, respect differing experiences, avoid harassment and discrimination, and respect privacy. Disagreement about technical decisions is welcome; personal attacks are not.

This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and discussions, and when someone represents the project publicly. The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

## Reporting

For a concern that needs confidential handling, email the maintainer at **ilia.imerlishvili@gmail.com**. This address is public in this document. Reports sent by email are not posted publicly by AIVV; ordinary email is not end-to-end encrypted, so do not include secrets or sensitive project data. Do not post another person's private information in a public issue.
